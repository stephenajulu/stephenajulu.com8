## The Hidden Costs of Digital Dependency

Many organizations operate under the assumption that if they pay for a SaaS subscription, their data is secure and their operations are guaranteed. However, an unmonitored reliance on external providers exposes you to several critical risks:

*   **Geopolitical and Regulatory Shifts**: Regulatory bodies are increasingly enforcing strict data residency rules (such as the Kenyan Data Protection Act or the GDPR). If your customer data is stored on foreign servers, you may be in violation of these laws, facing hefty fines.
*   **Shadow IT**: Employees frequently adopt unapproved third-party tools to make their jobs easier, unwittingly leaking proprietary source code, internal spreadsheets, and sensitive customer communications onto unmanaged external platforms.
*   **Vendor Termination & Account Lockout**: If a provider flags your account due to an automated false positive, or if their system suffers a catastrophic outage, your business operations can grind to a halt instantly, with no clear path to recovery.
*   **Arbitrary Pricing Hikes**: Subscription models are designed to hook you in. Once your data is deeply integrated into their system, vendors can increase subscription fees, leaving you with little choice but to pay.

A data sovereignty audit is the first step in identifying these vulnerabilities and taking back control of your operations.

## What We Audit: Core Components

My auditing methodology is comprehensive, covering all aspects of your technical operations and digital touchpoints:

### 1. SaaS & Vendor Dependency Mapping
I compile an inventory of every cloud service, API, software package, and hosting provider your company uses. We trace the flow of data across these systems, mapping out exactly where your business data is created, processed, and stored. This visual dependency map highlights single points of failure and vendor lock-in traps.

### 2. Data Leakage & Vulnerability Identification
I audit how your team shares files, communicates internally, and handles sensitive intellectual property. I look for common data leakage points—such as public cloud buckets, unencrypted emails, public code repositories, and unauthorized data sharing apps. We evaluate password policies, API key storage, and access control models.

### 3. Compliance and Data Residency Audits
I assess your systems against local data protection frameworks (such as the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner - ODPC in Kenya) and international standards (like GDPR). I verify if your storage systems comply with data localization mandates, ensuring you are shielded from regulatory audits and legal exposure.

### 4. Backup Integrity & Recovery Audits
A backup is only as good as its restore capability. I audit your backup infrastructure to confirm if backups are encrypted, stored in geographically isolated locations, and protected from ransomware. I run mock restoration tests to measure your Recovery Time Objective (RTO) and Recovery Point Objective (RPO).
