## 🔒 Direct and Sovereign Data
I believe in data sovereignty and individual privacy. Here is how I handle data on this website:

*   **No Analytics Cookies**: This site does not set tracking cookies. There is no Google Analytics or similar third-party tracking scripts.
*   **Form Submissions**: When you submit a message via the contact form or subscribe to the newsletter, the info you provide (email, name, message) is stored securely on Netlify. It is used solely to reply to you or send updates. It will never be sold, shared, or leased.
*   **Payments**: All payment processing is handled securely by Paystack. I do not store your credit card or mobile money details.
*   **Webmentions**: If you syndicate replies to this website using Webmentions, the profile picture, name, and comment you made public on Twitter, Mastodon, or your own site will be cached and displayed on the matching page.
